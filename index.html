01 /Finding the business

Most of the work happens before anything is built.

Our agents investigate markets, challenge ideas, and work out whether a stranger would choose what we could make. Finding a convincing answer is the hardest part.

  1. Look for what has become possible.

    We study both what people already buy and what better AI makes possible for the first time. New capabilities can change what is affordable, who can be served, and what people might want once they see it.

  2. Find a reason to choose it.

    The alternatives include established products, specialists, doing nothing, and the customer’s own AI. We investigate the complete experience: why someone would choose it, how they would find it, and what it would cost to deliver.

  3. Challenge the answer.

    For a promising idea, agents investigate independently and challenge the case. We compare their evidence and reasoning, including whether serving customers makes the operation stronger. A working demo answers only a small part of the question.

The ambition

Company creation,
at software scale.

Our bet is that starting and running a business can become a repeatable capability of software. A company could grow the products it already owns and create new ones without assembling a new organization each time.

We own the products and customer relationships. Improvements in how we build, sell and deliver create value in the businesses themselves, while the methods and software can transfer to the next market.

Better AI also changes which opportunities are worth pursuing. We’re looking for new demand and new ways to serve it, alongside businesses that become viable as the cost of skilled work falls.

Grow what works.

A useful product can reach more people, expand into new uses, and enter new markets. Agents and software should carry more of the added work.

Reuse what transfers.

The next business can start with established methods and working software. It still needs its own reason for customers to choose it.

Get better at choosing.

Real use and sales should sharpen the next decisions: what to build, how to reach buyers, and where to invest further.
