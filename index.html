Entrepreneurship
at a new scale.

We believe AI will change the economics of entrepreneurship. As AI takes on more research, software development and operations, a smaller organization can create better products and serve more customers. We’re designing Ithy around that shift.

One operating company can support many businesses. A new venture can begin with technology and operations already in place. Where AI lowers the cost of serving customers, it opens new price points and markets. Our ambition spans consumer and business demand worldwide, including new categories made possible by AI.

250+

Business ideas researched.

That makes discovery central to Ithy. We use AI to find unmet needs and emerging demand, then assess which could support a substantial business. Most of our research goes into this work. Our bet is that entrepreneurial judgment becomes more valuable as AI takes on more execution.

We own the businesses we create. Their revenue and long-term value accrue to Ithy. We intend to use revenue from successful businesses to expand them and launch new ventures. The technology and commercial knowledge carry forward, so each new venture can build on the businesses that came before it.

The goal: revenue and customer reach that grow faster than headcount.

Closing the gap between a need and the business that meets it.

Our long-term ambition is an economy that responds to people almost as quickly as their needs change. AI would continually discover opportunities, create products and build businesses around new or overlooked demand.

Ithy.com is our first business. We’re building Ithy to create and own substantial businesses in that future.
