Progress at the AI frontier expands the work a company can take on and the markets it can enter.

Our ambition spans consumer and business markets worldwide: established markets where a better product can win customers, and new categories made possible by advances in AI reasoning, software development and autonomous work.

Designed for
compounding growth.

We’re building a flywheel where successful businesses supply the resources and experience to create more of them.

For the right businesses, AI can lower the cost of delivery and operation, opening price points and markets that would be difficult to serve with conventional staffing. A new business can use the same technology and operating base.

Revenue from the businesses that succeed can be reinvested in better products and further launches. The technology and commercial experience they generate can improve the next venture.

The goal: revenue and customer reach that grow faster than headcount.

An advantage
that accumulates.

We put AI to work on opportunity discovery itself. The research and commercial knowledge we develop stay with Ithy.

Ithy.com has sold to customers since January 2025. It gives us experience in reaching customers through content, creators and paid acquisition, improving a subscription product and operating a live service.

We design businesses around AI from the start and build on advances from across the frontier. Experience in the markets we enter, and research into those we decline, inform each new choice.
