The research

Commercial judgment,
supplied by software.

Our agents develop and challenge the commercial case for each prospective business.

Each opportunity is weighed against the alternatives its customers already have: established products, specialists, existing workflows and their own AI. The case must explain how the product will earn a customer’s preference, how those customers can be reached, and whether the economics hold through delivery and ongoing operation.

Two research agents assess each opportunity independently, then reconcile their conclusions in writing. Disagreements prompt further investigation, comparison or a prototype designed to test a consequential assumption.

The ambition

Entrepreneurship as
a repeatable capability.

Our ambition is to expand the number and scale of businesses a single company can create and operate.

An individual product can grow into a substantial business. The research methods, engineering, customer-acquisition experience and operating software developed along the way can support subsequent businesses.

The larger possibility is entrepreneurial judgment that can serve many opportunities at once. Agents already conduct parallel investigations and much of Ithy.com’s operation. Extending that work could allow new businesses to share the same organization, without requiring a new founding team for each.

Ithy owns its businesses, so improvements in research and operations can add to their value as well as our capacity to enter further markets. Better AI also expands the opportunity set, including new products and experiences that were previously impractical.
